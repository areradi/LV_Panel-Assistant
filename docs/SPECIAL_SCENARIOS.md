## Calculation conventions

- UPS apparent power: `S = P / PF`; check both UPS kVA and kW.
- Preliminary battery capacity: `Ah = P(W) × t(h) / [Vdc × efficiency × usable_fraction]`. This linear estimate is not an autonomy certificate.
- PV cold open-circuit voltage: `Voc_cold = Ns × Voc_STC × [1 + beta(%/°C)/100 × (Tmin - 25°C)]` for the supported negative module coefficient and design-temperature range.
- PV current screen: `Ireq = Isc_module × Nparallel × supplied_design_factor`; the factor needs a documented basis.
- EV AC input: `I = Pout / (Kphase × Vac × PF × efficiency)`; `Kphase=1` for single phase using L-N voltage, `sqrt(3)` for balanced three phase using L-L voltage. Charger input voltage is entered explicitly, independently of the general panel supply voltage. DC output: `Idc=Pout/Vdc`.

## Enclosure form and results navigation

Floor-standing, wall-mounted, desk/console and rack are independent of MCC/distribution/control/pump-station electrical function. Mounting metadata can be stored in catalog entries. A mounting choice filters catalog candidates; absent matching data remains an estimate, not a verified product. Custom dimensions are optional and invalidate a catalog-product claim. Mechanical arrangement and mounting-specific heat dissipation still need manufacturer verification. IP solid and water performance are screened separately; immersion is not assumed to imply jet-water protection. See [ABB's IP explanation](https://library.e.abb.com/public/08ef4a9e2d124c0d90148569f89b95da/ABB-KAT.1%20E%20_7TCA292000R6653_01.2019_Poland.EN_Low%20res.pdf).

The Equipment workflow opens feeder/equipment results. The Busbars workflow opens separate incomer, phase/neutral/PE busbar, Icw/Ipk, support and voltage-drop results. Scenario findings appear in the design-validation log. Missing data is included in the displayed status count and prevents a special-scenario design-verification report from concluding PASS.
